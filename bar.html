SPIDER MAN FIRST SERIES

The Brilliant Scientist:

Norman Osborn was the cold, ambitious head of Oscorp Industries, a massive chemical and technology conglomerate, and the father of Peter Parker’s best friend, Harry Osborn.

The Goblin Formula

In an attempt to create a "super-soldier" serum to win a military contract, Norman tested an unstable chemical formula on himself, which granted him superhuman strength and intellect but shattered his sanity.

The Green Goblin

Developing a fractured second personality, he donned a ghoulish green costume and utilized advanced weaponry—like the Goblin Glider and Pumpkin Bombs—to take control of the New York underworld.

The Green Goblin

After discovering Peter’s secret identity, Osborn became Spider-Man's most personal enemy, leading to the infamous night at the Brooklyn Bridge that resulted in the death of Gwen Stacy.
