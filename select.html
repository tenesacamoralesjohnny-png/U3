SPIDER MAN FIRST SERIES

Superhuman Strength

Peter can lift several tons, allowing him to overpower most human adversaries, catch falling vehicles, and knock out opponents with a single blow.

Power and Responsibility

Peter Parker was a standout student at Midtown High School, possessing a genius-level intellect and a profound passion for science and technology.

Social Isolation:

He possesses the ability to mentally control the electrostatic force between his body and surfaces, allowing him to stick to and climb walls, ceilings, and other structures.

Spider-Sense:

A near-supernatural precognitive sense that triggers a tingling sensation at the base of his skull, alerting him to personal danger or incoming attacks before they happen.

Superhuman Agility and Reflexes:

His equilibrium and reaction times are dozens of times faster than a regular human's, enabling him to dodge bullets and perform complex acrobatic maneuvers mid-air.
