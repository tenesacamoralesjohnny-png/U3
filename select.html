Who is Peter Parker?

Peter Parker was a shy teenager living in Queens New York, he was passionate over photography and in love with Mary James. As Peter went on a school feild ttrip he got bitten my a radioactive spider

Abilities:None Human Strength

Academic Brilliance

Peter Parker was a standout student at Midtown High School, possessing a genius-level intellect and a profound passion for science and technology.

Social Isolation:

Despite his intelligence, he was a lonely outcast who faced constant harassment and bullying from his more popular peers, most notably Flash Thompson.

Family Devotion:

Raised by his loving Aunt May and Uncle Ben in Queens, Peter was a deeply compassionate and dutiful nephew who prioritized his family above all else.

Physical Frailty

Before his transformation, he was characterized by his slight build and lack of athletic ability, often feeling invisible to the world around him.
